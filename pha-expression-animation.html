Inducible expression of the phaA–phaB–phaC–peiR operon

A lac-regulated operon in E. coli BL21 stays silent until IPTG lifts the repressor. Transcription then yields the PHA biosynthesis enzymes, which build a bionanoparticle displaying the PeiR lytic enzyme on its surface.
